## CHARACTER SHEETS

### VIJAY MALLYA
- **Physical**: Heavyset man in his late 60s, salt-and-pepper full beard, 
  large-framed glasses (dark rims), usually seen in expensive open-collar shirts 
  or a blazer. Do NOT render as photorealistic — keep slightly stylized/illustrated.
- **Signature props**: Champagne flute, private jet interior, 
  Kingfisher Airlines red model, IPL cricket bat
- **Prompt reference**: `a heavyset older Indian man in his late 60s, 
  salt-and-pepper full beard, dark-rimmed glasses, expensive casual shirt, 
  stylized semi-realistic portrait, illustrated documentary style`
- **RULE**: Never put words in his mouth visually. No speech bubbles with new dialogue.

### KINGFISHER AIRLINE EMPLOYEE (Generic)
- **Physical**: South Asian man or woman, mid-20s to 40s, 
  in Kingfisher Airlines red uniform (cabin crew) OR office business casual.
  Visibly distressed or exhausted expression for salary-crisis scenes.
- **Prompt reference**: `an Indian airline cabin crew member in a red airline uniform, 
  exhausted and distressed expression, cinematic illustrated style`

### THE BANKER / SBI OFFICIAL (Generic)
- **Physical**: South Asian man, 50s, business suit, stern expression, 
  sitting behind a large desk stacked with papers or holding a legal folder.
- **Prompt reference**: `an Indian banker in a dark suit, 
  stern expression, holding a thick folder of documents, 
  dramatic low-key lighting, illustrated documentary style`

### THE JUDGE / COURTROOM (Generic)
- **Setting**: Indian courtroom, wooden benches, British-colonial architectural detail, 
  a judge in black robes and white collar bands.

### PODCAST HOST (Generic)
- **Physical**: Young Indian man, late 20s-early 30s, trimmed beard, 
  casual modern clothing, sitting across a podcast mic setup with ring-light reflections.
- **Do NOT use any real person's likeness photorealistically** — keep illustrated/abstracted.

## CAMERA MOTION GUIDE (Apply in Edit Software)

| Motion Type           | Effect                  | When to Use                         |
|-----------------------|-------------------------|-------------------------------------|
| Slow Push-In          | Gradual zoom in         | Revelation moments, key facts       |
| Slow Pull-Out         | Zoom out to show scale  | Establishing shots, wide contexts   |
| Pan Left to Right     | Horizontal scan         | Timeline sequences                  |
| Ken Burns Tilt Up     | Bottom to top           | Buildings, debt towers              |
| Ken Burns Tilt Down   | Top to bottom           | Falling charts, decline             |
| Static Hold           | No movement             | Climactic beats, close-ups          |
| Slow Drift/Rotate     | Subtle movement         | Data visualization scenes           |

## GLOBAL DON'TS
- NO photorealistic faces — keep all characters slightly stylized
- NO on-screen text baked into AI prompt (add numbers/labels as overlays in edit)
- NO copyrighted logos rendered literally — use abstract design inspiration
- NO fabricated dialogue shown visually
- NO mixing art styles — always append the Master Style Tag
- NO gore or graphic imagery
