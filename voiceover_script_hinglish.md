## [00:08] SCENE 1 — HOOK

"2nd March, 2016."

"Mumbai ke Chhatrapati Shivaji Airport pe ek aadmi plane pe chadhta hai."

"Uske paas return ticket nahi tha."

"Woh Indian banks ka hazaron crore rupaye le ke baitha tha."

"Aur Supreme Court ek din baad uska passport seize karne waali thi."

"Woh aadmi tha Vijay Mallya — jo kabhi apne aap ko 'King of Good Times' kehta tha."

"Aur naau saal baad, usne ek chaar ghante ka podcast diya — sirf yeh kehne ke liye ki woh chor nahi hai."

"Toh aaj hum ek-ek karke uske sab claims lenge — aur unhe documented facts ke saath compare karenge."

---

## [01:00] SCENE 2 — MALLYA KAUN THA?

"Iss story ko samajhne ke liye pehle yeh samajhna zaroori hai ki Vijay Mallya apni peak pe actually tha kaun."

"Sirf 28 saal ki umar mein, inhone apne baap se inherit kiya United Breweries — India ke sabse purane aur sabse bade alcohol conglomerates mein se ek."

"Unke leadership mein United Spirits duniya ki second-largest spirits company ban gayi volume ke hisaab se."

"IPL cricket team thi, Formula One team thi, multiple yachts the, aur private jet bhi."

"Woh Rajya Sabha ke member bhi the — yani Indian Parliament ke upper house mein seat thi unki. Aur unhone is diplomatic position ka pura faayda uthaya."

"2000s ke middle tak, yeh shayad India ka sabse flamboyant businessman tha."

"Media ne inhe 'King of Good Times' ka title diya — aur yeh banda iss title ko bade fakh se carry karta tha."

---

## [02:30] SCENE 3 — KINGFISHER KA SAPNA

"2005 mein Mallya ne launch ki Kingfisher Airlines."

"Positioning thi premium — ek 'fun airline,' inspired by Virgin Atlantic."

"Cabin mein leather seats the, personal entertainment screens the, economy class mein bhi chef-prepared khana."

"India mein kisi airline ne pehle aisa nahi kiya tha."

"2007 mein Mallya ne ek aisa move kiya jo ultimately airline ko barbaad kar dega — unhone Air Deccan acquire ki, ek struggling budget carrier, roughly 550 crore rupaye mein."

"Bhai, aviation ek brutally capital-intensive business hai. Fuel, salaries, maintenance, landing fees — yeh sab kabhi band nahi hote."

"Jaise hi passenger revenue dips karta hai, countdown shuru ho jaata hai."

"2010 tak Kingfisher Airlines cash bleed kar rahi thi jo sustain karna mushkil tha."

"Company ne already 1700 crore se zyada ka loss accumulate kar liya tha."

"Airline ne kabhi profit nahi banaya. Ek bhi saal mein nahi. Shuru se leke end tak."

---

## [04:00] SCENE 4 — THE LOANS

"Airline ko uda ke rakhne ke liye Mallya ne borrowing shuru ki."

"Aur sirf ek bank se nahi — satrah banks se."

"Akele State Bank of India ne Kingfisher Airlines ko 1600 crore se zyada diye."

"IDBI Bank ne karib 900 crore."

"Yeh choti raqam nahi thi — aur sabse important baat yeh hai ki yeh public ka paisa tha."

"Mallya ne yeh loans sirf ek corporate borrower ki tarah nahi liye the."

"Unhone personally guarantor ke roop mein sign kiya — matlab unka apna naam aur personal assets daanv pe the."

"2012 mein Kingfisher Airlines ka operating license finally suspend ho gaya."

"Employees ko mahino se salary nahi mili thi. Fuel suppliers ko payment nahi. Airport authorities ko fees nahi."

"Chaar hazaar se zyada employees achanak na job ke rahe, na salary ke — aur koi jawab dene waala bhi nahi."

"Loans par interest badh raha tha. Banks ne accounts ko Non-Performing Assets — NPAs — declare kar diya."

"Debt publicly acknowledge kiye gaye se kahin zyada tezi se badh raha tha."

---

## [06:00] SCENE 5 — CLAIM 1: "COURT NE SALARY FUNDS FREEZE KAR DIYE"

"Ab chalte hain Mallya ke claims ki taraf — podcast mein jo unhone kaha."

"Shuru karte hain unki pehli baat se — employees ko salary kyun nahi mili."

"Unka claim yeh hai: Karnataka High Court ne woh funds freeze kar diye the jo specifically employees ki salaries ke liye the."

"Toh galti court ki thi — unki nahi."

"Yeh sun ke logical lagta hai. Courts assets freeze karti hain — yeh sach hai."

"Toh dates check karte hain."

"Kingfisher Airlines ke employees ki salaries mid-2012 ke aas-paas rukni shuru huyin."

"Karnataka High Court ne relevant funds 2013 mein freeze kiye — pura ek saal baad."

"Woh court un funds ko freeze nahi kar sakti thi jo employees ko mile hi nahi."

"Sequence hi kaam nahi karta."

"Lekin ek aur baat hai."

"Jab Mallya ne Karnataka High Court mein petition daali, toh unhone sirf ek mahine ki salary ke liye funds release karne ki request ki."

"Us waqt tak total outstanding salaries karib aath mahine ki thi — roughly teen sau crore rupaye."

"Unhone sirf ek mahine ka maanga."

"Baaki saat mahine ka kya?"

"Aur yeh salary aaj bhi unpaid hai. Partially nahi. Mostly nahi. Poori tarah se unpaid."

---

## [08:00] SCENE 6 — BIRTHDAY PARTY

"Jab uske employees mahino se salary ka wait kar rahe the, Mallya ne Goa mein apna 60th birthday celebrate kiya."

"Ek aisi party ke saath jo reportedly India ki sabse mehenga private events mein se ek thi."

"Media reports ne party ki cost 13 crore rupaye se zyada estimate ki."

"International entertainers ko fly in karaya gaya. Guests ko five-star resorts mein rakha gaya."

"Podcast mein unki defence simple thi — Kingfisher Airlines unka sirf ek business tha. Unke paas aur bhi income streams the."

"Lekin yahan moral weight bahut bhaari hai."

"Yeh wahi aadmi hai jo baad mein claim karega ki usne apne employees ko bachane ki bahut koshish ki — aur wahi ek birthday party pe itna kharch kiya jitna uske employees ek decade mein nahi kamaate."

---

## [09:00] SCENE 7 — CLAIM 2: "MAIN SIRF GUARANTOR THA"

"Claim number do."

"Mallya kehte hain ki unhone personally koi loan nahi liya."

"Woh sirf guarantor the — aur unka argument hai ki guarantor hona debtor hone se alag hai."

"Technically, yeh sach hai."

"Loans Kingfisher Airlines Limited ke naam pe liye gaye the."

"Mallya ne personal guarantor ke roop mein sign kiya — primary borrower ke roop mein nahi."

"Lekin guarantor hone ka matlab law mein yeh hota hai: agar borrower default kare, toh guarantor fully liable ho jaata hai."

"Guarantee ka yahi toh poora point hai."

"Indian courts aur UK High Court dono ne rule kiya hai ki Mallya, personal guarantor ke roop mein, defaulted loans ki full amount ke liye legally liable hain."

"Yeh settled law hai — koi interpretation nahi."

"Yeh kehna ki 'main sirf guarantor tha' ek legal technicality hai jo outcome nahi badlti."

"Jab company default hui, liability unpe transfer ho gayi."

"Isliye toh unhone guarantee sign ki thi."

---

## [10:30] SCENE 8 — CLAIM 3: "KOI CRIMINAL ALLEGATION NAHI"

"Claim teen."

"Mallya kehte hain unka maamla fundamentally ek civil matter hai — ek unpaid debt — criminal case nahi."

"Woh khud ko ek failed businessman kehte hain, criminal nahi."

"Yeh claim seedha contradict hota hai Enforcement Directorate dwara — India ki financial crime investigation agency."

"ED ne Mallya pe Prevention of Money Laundering Act ke under charges daale hain."

"Seized bank records aur corporate filings ke basis pe, unka allegation hai ki loan funds Kingfisher Airlines se divert kiye gaye."

"Allegedly divert kiye gaye funds ka destination tha Force India — unki Formula One team — aur Royal Challengers Bangalore cricket franchise."

"ED ka yeh bhi allegation hai ki loans ke against pledged certain assets undervalued kiye gaye — matlab banks ko loan amounts se kum security mili."

"Yeh allegations civil court ki nahi hain kisi unpaid bill pe."

"Yeh criminal charges hain jo ek statutory law enforcement agency ne documentary evidence ke saath file kiye hain."

---

## [12:00] SCENE 9 — CLAIM 4: DEBT KI RAQAM

"Claim chaar."

"Mallya ek specific figure consistently quote karte hain — 6203 crore rupaye."

"2017 ke debt recovery tribunal ruling se liya gaya number."

"Alag alag banks aur media reports ne 9000 crore se lekar 14000 crore tak ke figures cite kiye hain."

"Mallya kehte hain yeh inflated hain."

"Toh sach kaun bolta hai?"

"Jawab yeh hai — dono figures sahi ho sakte hain."

"Depends karta hai ki debt kab calculate ki gayi."

"Wohi 2017 tribunal ruling jo Mallya quote karte hain, wahi specify bhi karti hai ki outstanding amount par 11.5 percent annual interest lag raha hai — 2013 se calculated."

"6203 crore pe 11.5 percent compound interest lagao 2013 se 2025 tak — yani baarah saal — total 20000 crore se zyada ho jaata hai."

"Zyada figures fabrication nahi hain. Yeh sirf maths hai."

"Mallya 2017 tribunal figure use karte hain — aur wahan ruk jaate hain."

"Usi document mein interest clause ka koi zikr nahi karte."

"Yeh selective quotation hai usi document ki jo woh khud apna evidence bataate hain."

---

## [13:30] SCENE 10 — CLAIM 5: "MAINE CHAAR BAAR SETTLE KARNE KI KOSHISH KI"

"Claim paanch."

"Mallya kehte hain unhone 2012 aur 2015 ke beech banks ko chaar alag settlement offers diye — sab reject ho gaye."

"Woh khud ko aisa dikhate hain jisne sab theek karne ki koshish ki — aur ignore kiya gaya."

"Yeh claim verify karna bahut aasaan hona chahiye."

"Is scale ke settlement offers newsworthy events hote hain — legal documentation chahiye hoti hai, board approvals, public filings."

"Research teams ne 2012 se 2015 ke period mein in chaar offers ki koi contemporaneous news coverage nahi dhundhi."

"Jo documented hai woh yeh hai — Mallya ke public settlement proposals 2016 mein shuru hue."

"Jab woh India chhod chuke the, aur Supreme Court action lene ki taiyari kar rahi thi."

"Ek aadmi jo saalo se settle karne ki koshish karta raha — aur ek aadmi jo settle karne ke offers tabhi shuru karta hai jab woh safely pahunch chuka ho desh se bahut door — in dono mein bahut bada fark hai."

---

## [15:00] SCENE 11 — THE ESCAPE

"March 2016."

"Supreme Court of India ek application sun ne waali thi Mallya ka passport seize karne ke liye."

"Yeh application State Bank of India ne file ki thi."

"Scheduled hearing se ek din pehle — 2nd March ko — Vijay Mallya London ki flight pe chale gaye."

"Unhone multiple interviews mein maintain kiya hai ki yeh deliberate escape nahi tha."

"Unka kehna hai ki London jaane ke legitimate business reasons the, aur unhe bilkul nahi pata tha ki banks unke passport ke against move karne waale hain."

"Timing — Supreme Court hearing se ek din pehle — zyaadatar logon ke liye coincidence maanna bahut mushkil raha hai."

---

## [16:00] SCENE 12 — UK EXTRADITION KI LADAI

"India ne February 2017 mein United Kingdom se Mallya ki extradition formally request ki."

"Jo follow hua woh tha British court system ke multiple tiers mein ek multi-year legal battle."

"December 2018 mein, Westminster Magistrates Court ke judge ne rule kiya ki Mallya ko extradite kiya jaana chahiye."

"Judge ko fraud aur money laundering ka prima facie case mila."

"Mallya ne appeal ki."

"UK High Court ne April 2020 mein extradition order uphold kiya."

"Unhone phir Supreme Court of UK mein appeal ki — aur July 2020 mein leave to appeal refuse ho gaya."

"2026 ke shuru tak, unki extradition execute nahi hui hai."

"Woh UK mein apni bankruptcy se related ongoing legal proceedings cite karte hain — argument yeh hai ki extradition se pehle yeh proceedings complete honi chahiye."

"Banks ne seized assets ke zariye outstanding debt ka karib 7000 crore rupaye recover kar liya hai — jisme United Spirits mein unki stake, properties, aur doosri holdings shaamil hain."

---

## [17:30] SCENE 13 — CURRENT STATUS 2026

"Toh 2026 mein aaj situation kahan hai?"

"Mallya United Kingdom mein rehte hain, jahan woh simultaneously extradition aur bankruptcy proceedings dono ko contest kar rahe hain."

"Indian courts ne unhe wilful defaulter declare kiya hai."

"Indian agencies ne multiple jurisdictions mein unki properties aur assets attach ki hain."

"Unka Indian passport expire ho chuka hai."

"Aur Kingfisher Airlines mein kaam karne waale employees ko aaj bhi unki unpaid wages ka koi resolution nahi mila."

"Kuch retire ho gaye. Kuch guzar gaye."

"Unka yeh debt officially kisi ki priority list mein nahi hai."

---

## [18:30] SCENE 14 — "PAISA WAAPIS AAYA MATLAB MAIN CHOR NAHI"

"Podcast mein Mallya ka sabse striking argument yeh hai."

"Banks ne paisa ka ek bada hissa recover kar liya hai — isliye woh traditional sense mein defaulter nahi hain — aur certainly chor toh bilkul nahi."

"Iss logic ko thoda dhyaan se socho."

"Agar koi bank loot karta hai, aur police stolen money recover kar leti hai — toh kya money recover hone ka matlab yeh hai ki robbery hui hi nahi?"

"Act aur recovery — yeh legally alag events hain."

"Courts innocence assess nahi karte iss basis pe ki assets eventually seize hue ya nahi."

"Woh assess karte hain ki original conduct fraudulent tha ya criminal."

"Aur ek baat — banks aur government agencies dwara assets seizure, Mallya ke voluntarily debt repay karne se alag hai."

"Assets liye gaye — returned nahi kiye gaye."

---

## [19:30] SCENE 15 — CONCLUSION

"Vijay Mallya ek complex figure hai."

"Unhone real cheezein banai — airlines, sports teams, brands."

"Hazaron logo ko employ kiya."

"Lekin documented facts yeh support nahi karte jo narrative woh abhi bech rahe hain."

"Salaries court ke kuch freeze karne se pehle nahi di gayi thi."

"Guarantor clause legally binding hai."

"Criminal charges real aur documented hain."

"Debt figures compound interest se explain hote hain — usi document mein jo woh cite karte hain."

"Aur settlement offers ka koi contemporaneous record nahi hai."

"Chaar hazaar employees aaj bhi us salary ka intezaar kar rahe hain jo unhone ek decade se zyada pehle earn ki thi."

"Yeh fact nahi badlta — chahe yeh story kaise bhi sunai jaaye."
